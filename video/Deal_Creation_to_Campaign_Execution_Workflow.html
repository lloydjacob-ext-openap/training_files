How partners measure
Linear
1OpenAP sends each partner an Expression in its own IDs: Nielsen, VideoAmp, Comscore, 605
2Partner matches the audience against its viewing data for the airings in the ARL
3Calculates reach, impressions, and frequency
Digital
1Partners receive impression-level exposure records, translated into their own IDs
2Partner measures reach and frequency directly from those exposures
Linear: exposure inferred from viewing data and the airing schedule. Digital: one record per impression.

Recap
1Deal

Sold once, can include multiple publishers, and runs as one campaign per publisher.

2Identity solution

Each publisher receives its own Expression, which ties back to the Audience Record.

3Distribution

Direct, FreeWheel, or intermediaries. Then the campaign runs.

4Delivery logs

ARLs for linear, impression records for digital.

5Mapping

Delivery tied back to the right OpenAP campaign.

6Measurement

Resolved, translated to partner IDs, measured, and posted.

Results are measured against the same Audience Record the campaign was built on.
